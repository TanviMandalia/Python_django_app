Quick Health Tips

Essential daily guidance to protect your joints, spine, and mobility

1

Back Pain Relief

“Don’t ignore back pain that lasts more than a few days. Early physiotherapy assessment can prevent long-term problems.”

2

Neck & Posture Care

“Keep your screen at eye level and take a 2-minute stretch break every 30–45 minutes to reduce neck stiffness.”

3

Sciatica Care

“Pain traveling from the lower back to the leg may indicate nerve irritation. Proper diagnosis and exercises help faster recovery.”

4

Knee Pain Prevention

“Strengthening your thigh and hip muscles helps support your knees and reduces stress during daily activities.”

5

Shoulder Health

“Avoid ignoring shoulder stiffness. Early mobility exercises can prevent worsening of movement restriction.”

6

Exercise Tip

“Correct exercise heals — wrong exercise can increase pain. Always follow a personalized rehab plan.”

7

Senior Citizen Care

“Regular balance and strength training reduces fall risk and keeps you independent.”

8

Pain Awareness

“Painkillers may reduce symptoms temporarily, but finding and treating the root cause gives long-term relief.”
